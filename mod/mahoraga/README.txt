Mahoraga: Adapt to Anything - a Noita mod (version 1.4.1)

Mahoraga, the Eight-Handled Wheel, waits in a temple arena carved into the edge wall
at the far east of the world: cross the desert eastwards until you reach the great
black wall; the arena doorway sits at ground level. Every parallel world has its own
arena and its own Mahoraga, tougher the further out you go.

- 8000 health, and like the game's own bosses he takes only half damage. His weakest hit deals 50
  damage, everything else in proportion.
- He adapts to anything: take enough of one kind of damage and his wheel turns and he
  becomes immune to it. Drowning counts too: he needs air until he adapts to it.
- Every adaptation heals him to full, raises his max health by 50% and all his damage by 50%.
- Fire burns in rapid ticks, so it takes much longer to adapt to (for him and for you).
- Destructive attacks: Sword of Extermination slashes that cut through everything that
  isn't indestructible, Crushing Leap, Unstoppable Charge, and (below half health) Wheel Pulse.
- New techniques the wheel teaches him:
  adapted to melee       -> Giant Slash: a huge slash that carves through anything in front of him
  adapted to fire        -> Giant Napalm: a fire bomb that sets a large area ablaze
  adapted to explosions  -> Giant Nuke: after a warning, an instant nuclear blast around him
  adapted to electricity -> Electrocute Giant Punch: an electric blast right in front of him
  adapted to holy power  -> Holy Shower: holy lances rain over a large area for a whole minute
  adapted to slicing     -> World Cutting Slash: about 3 seconds of huge white cuts across the whole area
  adapted to curses      -> Omega Black Hole: a giant black hole hangs above his head for 10 seconds
  adapted to impacts     -> Earthquake: the ground shakes and cracks, hitting everything standing near him
  adapted to materials   -> Magma Torrent: three globs of molten rock that leave pools of lava
  (the Electrocute Giant Punch is now a much bigger electric explosion)
- He adapts to every curse (the sun, the cursed rock, black holes, cursed hits...) and to harmful
  materials (lava, acid, toxic gas...).
- He adapts to your defenses: when 4 of his attacks in a row leave you unharmed, he studies why
  and overcomes it (each one is a full adaptation, with healing and doubling):
  projectile repulsion/slowing fields -> his attacks ignore them
  energy shields                      -> every attack drains your shields
  protections and immunities          -> part of every hit lands as curse damage
                                         (and he learns Cursed Wheel: rings of curse orbs)
  invisibility                        -> he no longer needs to see you
  nothing else explains it (evasion)  -> faster attacks and projectiles
                                         (and he learns Phantom Step: he blinks behind you and slashes)
- Some adaptations change him too: adapted to projectiles he raises two energy shields
  (like Skoude's); adapted to drowning he swims freely and fast.
- Rewards (take both): the perks
  "Adapt to Anything": each kind of harm you keep taking hurts you less: 50%, then 75%, and
  at the third step you are fully adapted and it can no longer harm you. Drowning included
  (you hold your breath longer, then no longer need air). Every NEW kind of harm you adapt
  to also makes you stronger: +50% max health and +50% damage on everything you shoot,
  compounding (x1.5, x2.25, x3.38...).
  "Summon Mahoraga": press your summon key (default G, change it in Options > Mod settings)
  to call your own Mahoraga, and press it again to dismiss him. He fights everything but you,
  and his explosions can't hurt you.
  He is a copy of the boss: when called he takes every kind of harm you have adapted to
  (with Adapt to Anything), with the same doubling of health and damage for each one and the
  techniques they unlock, and he keeps taking your new adaptations while he is out.

Divine Dogs perk (in the normal perk pool): press your Divine Dogs key (default H, Options >
Mod settings) to call the white Divine Dog, and again to send it back. It bites whatever is in
front of it (200 health) and dashes at enemies farther away, in any direction, to claw them
(Totality claws twice, with a bigger swipe). Every 25 kills, or any boss it helps kill, the dogs grow: the black dog
joins (both stronger), then they merge into Divine Dog: Totality (5x the base). If they fall,
they can be called again after 30 seconds, keeping their growth.

Escape from Mahoraga (Options > Mod settings, for new games): Mahoraga leaves his arena as your
run begins and digs straight at you, reaching you in about 10 minutes and never stopping after
that. You start with only one perk, the Divine Dogs. The chasing Mahoraga has a quarter of the usual
health and damage. Kill him to end the chase: he drops no perks, but returns to his arena at the
eastern edge, full strength, and drops them there as usual when beaten.

Single player. Fan-made boss inspired by Mahoraga from Jujutsu Kaisen by Gege Akutami;
not affiliated with the series or with Nolla Games. All sprites were drawn for this mod (Mahoraga animated in Aseprite).
Sounds and effects come from the player's own copy of Noita.

Installing by hand: copy this folder to Noita/mods/mahoraga and enable it in Mods,
or subscribe to it on the Steam Workshop.
